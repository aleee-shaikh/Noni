🏠 Real Use Cases

Telemedicine Support

Enables patients and attendants to connect with doctors remotely for medical consultations and communication.

Disinfection & Infection Control

Helps reduce the spread of bacteria and viruses by supporting hospital disinfection processes through autonomous movement, remote operation, and smart sanitization assistance in critical areas.

Monitoring & Surveillance

Provides real-time monitoring and surveillance of hospital environments to improve safety, security, and operational awareness, especially for ICU patients and baby incubators.

Hospital Guidance & Promotions

Assists visitors with navigation inside hospitals and supports awareness campaigns, announcements, and promotions.

Food & Medicine Ordering

Allows patients and attendants to order food and medicine directly through the robot system.

Companionship & Entertainment

Acts as a companion robot providing conversation, music, news updates, and emotional interaction.

Delivery Assistance

Delivers items such as medicines, documents, or supplies within hospital environments.

Smart Charging & Connectivity

Includes built-in charging ports and provides internet access support for connected operations.
