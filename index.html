Core Capabilities

Intelligence in Motion.

NONI combines autonomous navigation, AI interaction, computer vision and connected robotics into one intelligent service platform.

01 / NAVIGATION

Autonomous Navigation

Intelligent navigation allows NONI to move through service environments while detecting and avoiding obstacles.

02 / AI

AI-Powered Assistant

Conversational AI enables NONI to understand requests, answer questions and assist users with intelligent interactions.

03 / VOICE

Voice Interaction

Users can communicate naturally with NONI through voice-based commands and conversational interaction.

04 / VISION

Computer Vision

Vision-based perception helps NONI understand its surroundings and identify objects and obstacles.

05 / DELIVERY

Autonomous Delivery

NONI can transport food, documents, medicines and other small items between designated locations.

06 / CONNECTIVITY

Smart Connectivity

Connected systems support remote monitoring, communication and intelligent service operations.

07 / GUIDANCE

Visitor Guidance

NONI can assist visitors with directions, information and interactive guidance.

08 / MONITORING

Remote Monitoring

Live camera and remote control capabilities provide operational visibility when human supervision is needed.
